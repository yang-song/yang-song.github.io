I am the Research Principal at Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL), where I work with Chief Scientist [Shengjia Zhao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=bMoauM4AAAAJ&hl=en) to help shape the lab's research direction.

During my Ph.D., I developed some of the core concepts and techniques behind score-based diffusion models, which now power many generative AI systems for images, video, and audio. You can read more about this line of work in a [blog post](/blog/2021/score/) or a [Quanta Magazine article](https://www.quantamagazine.org/the-physics-principle-that-inspired-modern-ai-art-20230105/). My research has been recognized with an [ICLR Outstanding Paper Award](https://iclr-conf.medium.com/announcing-iclr-2021-outstanding-paper-awards-9ae0514734ab) and Ph.D. fellowships from Apple and J.P. Morgan.

Before joining Meta, I led the strategic explorations team at OpenAI. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from [Stanford University](https://www.stanford.edu), advised by [Stefano Ermon](https://cs.stanford.edu/~ermon).
