I am the Research Principal at Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL), where I work with Chief Scientist [Shengjia Zhao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=bMoauM4AAAAJ&hl=en) to help shape the lab's research direction.

Much of my research career has been about turning noise into data. During my Ph.D. I worked on score-based diffusion models, a family of ideas that now sit under the hood of many generative AI systems for images, video, and audio. I like to think I asked a few of the right questions early; many talented people have since supplied far better answers. If you are curious, a [blog post](/blog/2021/score/) and a [Quanta Magazine article](https://www.quantamagazine.org/the-physics-principle-that-inspired-modern-ai-art-20230105/) tell the story better than I can here. Along the way, this work picked up an [ICLR Outstanding Paper Award](https://iclr-conf.medium.com/announcing-iclr-2021-outstanding-paper-awards-9ae0514734ab) and Ph.D. fellowships from Apple and J.P. Morgan.

Before joining Meta, I led the strategic explorations team at OpenAI. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from [Stanford University](https://www.stanford.edu), where [Stefano Ermon](https://cs.stanford.edu/~ermon) patiently advised me.
